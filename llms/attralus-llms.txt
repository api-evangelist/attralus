# Attralus

> Attralus is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in San Francisco, California
> developing pan-amyloid removal (PAR) therapeutics for patients with systemic amyloidosis.
> Its lead candidate, zamubafusp alfa (AT-02), is a pan-amyloid depleter fusion protein in a
> Phase 1/2 open-label extension study with U.S. FDA Orphan Drug Designation for AL amyloidosis.
